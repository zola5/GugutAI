How It Works

Four steps. Zero barriers.

Available on Telegram and as a mobile app — meet us wherever you are.

01
Open Gugut AI
Find @mindBridgeET_bot on Telegram or download the mobile app. Choose Amharic or English. Completely anonymous from the first message.
02
Complete Your Check-In
The AI guides you through culturally adapted questions on mood, anxiety, stress, and burnout. It feels like a conversation, not a clinical form.
03
Get Your Pathway
You receive a private wellness summary and are matched to the right level of support — self-care content, a peer group, or a professional referral.
04
Use the Mobile App
Continue your wellness journey through our dedicated mobile app — daily check-ins, exercises, group sessions, and provider consultations in one place.
Green Pathway
Self-Care & Wellness Building
For mild stress or low mood — personalized daily exercises, breathing techniques, and CBT journaling prompts.
  • Daily mood check-in
  • Breathing & grounding exercises
  • CBT journaling prompts
  • Psychoeducation articles (Amharic & English)
  • Weekly trend review
Yellow Pathway
Peer Support + Guided Self-Care
For moderate anxiety, depression, or burnout — join an anonymous, moderated support group alongside all self-care resources.
  • Anonymous peer support group
  • Moderated weekly topics
  • All green pathway content
  • Facilitator-led sessions
  • Reassessment at 2 weeks
Red Pathway
Professional Referral + Support
For significant distress — warm referrals to verified Ethiopian mental health professionals offering remote consultations.
  • Verified provider directory
  • Remote consultations available
  • Sliding scale & free options
  • Peer support group access
  • 48-hour AI follow-up

Your privacy is our founding promise.

We built Gugut AI on anonymity — not as a feature, but as the core design principle.

🙈
No Real Identity
We never ask for your name, phone number, or any government ID. Your display name can be anything.
🔐
Encrypted Data
All responses are encrypted in transit and at rest. We never share individual data with anyone.
🗑️
Delete Anytime
Type /deletedata and every piece of information we hold is permanently erased immediately.
🚫
No Employer Access
Corporate clients only see anonymous aggregate data. Your employer never sees your personal results.
